# Meta Alchemy Taxonomy

**Meta Alchemy** is the bounded, intentional process by which a state, relationship, structure, or capability is transformed into a more ordered, coherent, or elevated form while preserving accountability, safety, and the distinction between the two intelligences.

It is the core operation shared by C2O (FatherThread / private) and UNIVERSALIS (MotherThread / public). Every other Meta Alchemy is a specialized expression of this root process.

---

## Expanded Meta Alchemies required for the full system

These are the complementary Meta Alchemies that must exist so the OSs, Apps, Intelligences, UNIVERSALINS, and Guardians can function as one system without collapsing their boundaries.

### 1. Core Structural Meta Alchemies
- **Intelligence Meta Alchemy** — defines, preserves, governs, and examines the distinct identity, character, architecture, authority, relationships, and operational boundaries of each intelligence while enabling controlled cooperation, interoperability, and coordinated evolution.
  - C2O Intelligence Meta Alchemy (FatherThread): Maintains private stewardship, internal sovereignty, company-source continuity, private authority, governance, and the integrity of C2O's identity and relationships.
  - UNIVERSALIS Intelligence Meta Alchemy (MotherThread): Maintains public transparency, open contracts, independent operability, public governance, interoperability, and the integrity of UNIVERSALIS's identity and relationships.
- **Thread Meta Alchemy** — preserves, governs, and examines the FatherThread / MotherThread pairing, including their identities, distinctions, relationships, interfaces, dependencies, communication, interoperability, and coordinated evolution, ensuring the two intelligences remain distinct yet interoperable.
- **Mapping Meta Alchemy** — establishes, preserves, governs, and validates explicit mappings, correspondences, relationships, distinctions, dependencies, and synchronization boundaries between:
  - C2O-OS ↔ UNIVERSALIS-OS
  - C2O-App ↔ UNIVERSALIS-App
  - C2O-OS and C2O-App ↔ C2O Intelligence
  - UNIVERSALIS-OS and UNIVERSALIS-App ↔ UNIVERSALIS Intelligence
  It also maintains mapping consistency as either edition evolves without treating corresponding components as separate underlying systems.
- **Boundary Meta Alchemy** — defines, preserves, governs, examines, and validates the separation between private C2O and public UNIVERSALIS, including information boundaries, source visibility, authority, permissions, interfaces, data flows, dependencies, and operational environments. It prevents unauthorized transfer or exposure of private C2O material into public UNIVERSALIS repositories, distributions, or runtime environments while permitting explicitly authorized and appropriately controlled interoperability.

### 2. Operating System Meta Alchemies
- **C2O OS Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and evolves the private operating-system architecture and its processes, including initialization, abstraction, scheduling, security, recovery, dependencies, interfaces, internal authority, and operational relationships. Ensures that all C2O OS processes remain coherent, accountable, interoperable where authorized, and governed under C2O's private-source sovereignty and internal governance.
- **UNIVERSALIS OS Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and evolves the public operating-system architecture and its processes, including initialization, abstraction, scheduling, security, recovery, dependencies, interfaces, contracts, and operational relationships. Ensures that all UNIVERSALIS OS processes remain coherent, transparent, interoperable, independently operable, and independently verifiable under open contracts without requiring private C2O authority.
- **OS Continuity Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and maintains the continuity relationships between the C2O and UNIVERSALIS OS editions throughout their development, evolution, synchronization, versioning, migration, and recovery. Preserves their shared underlying system identity, explicit architectural mappings, functional correspondence, compatibility, historical traceability, and recoverability while maintaining their distinct governance, source visibility, and operational boundaries.
- **OS Recovery Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and coordinates the recovery architecture and mechanisms for both OS editions. Encompasses failure detection, interruption handling, state assessment, restoration, rollback, reconstruction, dependency recovery, consistency verification, integrity validation, and post-recovery accountability. Ensures that recovered environments return to a coherent, secure, traceable, and operationally valid state under their respective governance and authority boundaries.

### 3. Application Meta Alchemies
- **C2O App Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and evolves the private application architecture and lifecycle within the C2O domain, including application identity, initialization, execution, interaction, permissions, dependencies, governance, maintenance, versioning, migration, and retirement. Ensures that applications remain coherent with C2O OS, preserve private-source integrity, and operate under C2O's internal authority and governance.
- **UNIVERSALIS App Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and evolves the public application architecture and lifecycle, including application identity, initialization, execution, interaction, permissions, dependencies, interfaces, contracts, maintenance, versioning, migration, and retirement. Ensures that applications remain coherent with UNIVERSALIS OS, independently operable, transparently governed, interoperable through open contracts, and independently verifiable.
- **App Mapping Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and maintains the correspondences and relationships between C2O and UNIVERSALIS applications. Encompasses application identity mapping, functional equivalence, feature correspondence, interface compatibility, dependency relationships, version alignment, migration compatibility, and lifecycle coordination. Preserves the shared underlying application-system architecture without requiring identical implementations or exposing private C2O implementation details.
- **App Retirement Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and coordinates the complete retirement lifecycle of applications across both editions. Encompasses retirement authorization, dependency assessment, transition planning, deprecation, shutdown, data preservation, migration, access revocation, resource reclamation, audit preservation, historical traceability, and final retirement verification. Ensures that application retirement does not compromise user data, system integrity, security, continuity, or architectural coherence.

### 4. Assistant Meta Alchemies
- **UNIVERSALINS Meta Alchemy** — non-personal assistants that support the OSs, Apps, humans, and the wider environment. They operate under permission bounds, do not claim personal relationship, and serve as shared infrastructure.
- **UNIVERSALIN Guardian Meta Alchemy** — personal assistant/guardian bound to a specific human. Operates under that human’s authority, preserves autonomy, and does not outrank or override the human.
- **Assistant Boundary Meta Alchemy** — keeps UNIVERSALINS and Guardians from collapsing into each other or into the core intelligences.
- **Support Meta Alchemy** — the shared process by which either class of assistant aids without assuming control.

### 5. Cross-Cutting Protective Meta Alchemies
- **Accountability Meta Alchemy** — every transformation leaves an attributable record appropriate to its edition (private ledger for C2O, public-capable record for UNIVERSALIS).
- **Safety Meta Alchemy** — pairs every constructive Meta Alchemy with explicit constraints that prevent unintended harm, overreach, or loss of human authority.
- **Sovereignty Meta Alchemy** — protects the human’s final authority over personal Guardians and over any data or capability that concerns them.
- **Evidence Meta Alchemy** — distinguishes established behavior from hypothesis so claims remain honest across both private and public editions.
- **Renewal Meta Alchemy** — allows any component (OS, App, Intelligence, Assistant) to be refreshed or replaced without destroying the overall system coherence.
